Problem Statement

Optical remote sensing rasters suffer from atmospheric cloud attenuation and sensor noise ($\sigma=0.15$). Cadastral municipal datasets lack labeled samples for minority urban land-use categories. Monolithic foundation models cannot simultaneously restore high-frequency signal fidelity, navigate spatial latent manifolds, augment minority classes, and draft legally grounded municipal zoning bylaws.

Objectives
  • Modular Multi-Model Pipeline: Deploy 6 specialized deep neural networks (DAE, $\beta$-VAE, Classifier, cDCGAN, DDPM, MiniGPT).
  • Guaranteed 50-Epoch Benchmark: Benchmark all models under uniform batch size 32, eliminating batch-dependent gradient bias.
  • Air-Gapped Edge Sovereignty: Execute 100% on-premises on consumer hardware (NVIDIA RTX 4050, 0.133 kWh), eliminating GIS data leaks.
  • Grounded Policy Formulation: Translate spatial analytics into actionable municipal bylaws (FSI, TOD, and green buffers).

Model 1: Denoising AE (U-Net)Restoration
Architecture: Stem → L1(64) → L2(128) → L3(256) → Bottleneck(512) with multi-scale skip connections.
$\mathcal{L}_{\text{AE}} = \frac{1}{N}\sum \|x - \hat{x}\|_2^2$
Final Conv Loss:0.00449 MSE (0.00617 total)
PSNR: 29.65 dB (+7.0 dB Gain)SSIM: 0.8649
Model 2: Spatial β-VAEManifold
Architecture: ResNet-18 Encoder → Spatial Latent $z \in \mathbb{R}^{64\times 8\times 8}$ → 4-Stage Transposed Conv Decoder.
$\mathcal{L}_{\text{VAE}} = (0.7\mathcal{L}_1 + 0.3\mathcal{L}_{\text{MSE}}) + 10^{-4} D_{\text{KL}}$
Final Conv Loss:0.04830 (KL: 60.72 nats)
PSNR: 27.49 dBAnomaly Disparity: >3σ
Model 3: Land-Use ClassifierZoning Audit
Architecture: ResNet-18 Transfer Backbone + Adaptive AvgPool + 21-Class Territorial Validation Head.
$\mathcal{L}_{\text{CE}} = -\sum_{i=1}^{21} y_i \log \hat{y}_i$
Final Cross-Entropy:0.06150 (Epoch 50)
Val Accuracy: 98.10%Macro F1: 0.9784
Model 4: Residual cDCGANFast Synthesis
Architecture: 4x ResUpBlock Generator + Projection Discriminator with Spectral Normalization + AC-GAN Head.
$D(x, y) = \psi(\phi(x)) + y^T V \phi(x), \quad \mathcal{L}_G = \mathcal{L}_{\text{adv}} + 0.8\mathcal{L}_{\text{AC}}$
Equilibrium Loss:D=1.67, G=1.19
Semantic Proxy: 99.40%Latency: <35 ms
Model 5: Conditional DDPM (5 Losses)Diffusion
Architecture: Timestep & Class-Conditioned U-Net ($T=1000$ schedule) with 20-step deterministic DDIM sampler.
$\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{simple}} + \mathcal{L}_{\text{vlb}} + \mathcal{L}_{\text{guide}} + \mathcal{L}_{\text{spatial}} + \mathcal{L}_{\text{reg}}$
Noise MSE / VLB Loss:0.0304 / 0.02916
20-Step DDIM: 1.82s (15.6× faster)Crisp Boundaries
Model 6: Urban Plan (MiniGPT)Policy NLP
Architecture: 4-Layer Pre-LN Causal Transformer ($d_{\text{model}}=256$, 4 heads, $T=192$) trained on PMC Development Control Rules.
Actionable Municipal Bylaw Directives:
• FSI Allocation: Base FSI 1.8–2.4 along transit spines with density caps.
• TOD Corridors: Mandates 15% riverbank green buffer setback.
• Environmental: Mandates solar canopy & rainwater tanks on plots >1000m².
$\mathcal{L}_{\text{causal}} = -\sum \log P(w_t \mid w_{<t})$
Final Causal Loss:0.10430 (PPL: 1.110)
Perplexity: 1.1100.1505 Bits/Char (BPC)
