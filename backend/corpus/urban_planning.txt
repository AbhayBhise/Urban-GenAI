UrbanGen AI drafts advisory recommendations for planner review; they are not approvals.
===

Sustainable urban planning balances land, water, mobility, energy, and equity together, not one at a time.
===

Every recommendation below is a starting point for professional and community review.
===

Good plans protect ecological structure first, then arrange development around it.
===

Context: Pune's PMC area is about 516 sq km with a population near 7.4 million in 2026, projected to reach 10 million by 2041.
Recommendations:
- Align ward plans with Pune Metro Phase 1 station catchments and the Mula-Mutha riverfront project.
- Treat the Mula and Mutha river edges as a continuous ecological and public corridor, not a back boundary.
- Direct growth pressure from the Hinjewadi IT corridor into transit-served, mixed-use nodes rather than car-dependent sprawl.
- Protect the hill slopes and biodiversity parks that structure the city's drainage and microclimate.
===

Context: The Hinjewadi IT corridor draws large daily commuting flows into a car-oriented layout.
Recommendations:
- Add high-frequency mass transit and feeder buses before approving further office floor-space.
- Require campuses to open through-streets, footpaths, and ground-floor amenities to the public.
- Build worker housing within walking distance of employment to cut commute distance.
- Retain and connect the remaining green and water features as a campus-scale greenway.
===

Context: a heritage core with about 34% built-up area, roughly 12% green cover, a sparse road grid, no mapped drainage, and low population density.
Recommendations:
- Raise tree-canopy cover toward 25% using native, low-water species along streets and in pocket parks.
- Keep the historic street pattern and plot rhythm; infill must respect scale and materials.
- Reserve the floodplain as public open space and prohibit new habitable construction within it.
- Require rooftop solar readiness and shading design for all new buildings.
===

Context: a transit-station catchment with about 63% built-up area, roughly 3% green cover, a well-connected arterial network, no mapped drainage, and moderate population density.
Recommendations:
- Keep the historic street pattern and plot rhythm; infill must respect scale and materials.
- Guarantee that every household is within 400 m of a park, a school, and a primary health centre.
- Create a linear greenway connecting fragmented open spaces into one continuous network.
- Require rooftop solar readiness and shading design for all new buildings.
- Permit mixed use by right so shops, workspaces, and homes can share a street.
===

Context: a lakefront zone with about 71% built-up area, roughly 9% green cover, a dense but congested road grid, a single choked nala, and moderate population density.
Recommendations:
- Require 20% of every large plot to be kept as permeable, planted ground.

- Cap and price car parking; convert freed kerb space to footpath, planting, and cycle lanes.
- Keep the historic street pattern and plot rhythm; infill must respect scale and materials.
- Set a minimum share of small units and require a portion of affordable housing in large projects.

Recommendations:
- Allow gentle density (ground-plus-three to ground-plus-five) with active ground-floor frontages.
- Adopt a cool-roof and light-pavement standard to cut the local heat-island effect.
- Replace impervious paving in public right-of-way with permeable surfaces where the water table allows.
- Guarantee that every household is within 400 m of a park, a school, and a primary health centre.
